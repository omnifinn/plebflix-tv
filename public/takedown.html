Takedown

Plebflix TV does not host video files. Tapes play through an embed of a YouTube or Internet Archive upload, or the glass links out to the original page. If you are the rights holder and you want a tape removed from the broadcast, email takedown@plebflix.com.

Include the address of the tape on plebflix.com (or its title and channel), the original you own, and a way to confirm you hold the rights. We remove the tape from the shelf promptly.

Taking a tape off this site does not remove it from YouTube or the Internet Archive. Those requests go to the service that hosts the file.
